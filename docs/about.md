# About Prometheus Path

## The Idea

Prometheus stole fire from the gods and gave it to humanity. This project does the same with AI knowledge — taking what's locked behind university walls and expensive courses, and making it accessible to everyone.

## Learn in Public

This project follows the **Learn in Public** philosophy:

- Learning is tracked openly and transparently
- Insights and notes are shared as they happen
- Others are invited to fork, follow along, and contribute
- Mistakes and struggles are part of the journey, not hidden away

## How to Use This

**For self-learners:** Follow the syllabus at your own pace. Use the checkboxes to track your progress — it's saved in your browser, no account needed.

**For contributors:** Found a better free resource? Have a suggestion? Open an issue or PR on [GitHub](https://github.com/B3nW00d6r00v3/prometheus-path).

## Tech

Built with [MkDocs Material](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/), hosted on GitHub Pages. Progress tracking uses localStorage — your data stays on your machine.
